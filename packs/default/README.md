# Lucy's built-in sprite pack

Lucy's default look: an original kawaii cyberpunk netrunner, at 256×256.

The art was made with AI image models and reviewed by hand:

- The base picture was drawn with [FLUX.1 [schnell]](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell)
  (Apache-2.0).
- Each expression (blinking, talking, thinking, happy, error, listening) was edited from that
  picture with [FLUX.1 Kontext [dev]](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-Kontext-dev),
  whose licence lets its outputs be used for any purpose. Only her face is taken from each edit,
  so nothing else moves between frames.
- The top of her head, which ran off the first picture, was drawn in with Kontext too.
- The background was removed and the frames scaled down afterwards.

The frames are part of Lucy and covered by her licence (MIT OR Apache-2.0).

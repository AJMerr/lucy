# Lucy

Lucy is a personal AI assistant that works with any model: one running on your own computer
(Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp…) or a hosted one (OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Qwen…). She has a
netrunner's attitude, but facts, clean code and plain safety warnings come first, and `/plain`
turns the character off.

Talk to her in a terminal, in her app's window, or through her **sprite**, a small animated
figure who lives on your desktop and answers in speech bubbles. She can:

- **use your computer, with your say-so:** read and search files, run commands, edit code and
  fetch pages, asking before anything that changes something;
- **work through tasks on her own** in agent mode, within limits you set;
- **use more tools** through MCP servers: Gmail and Calendar, GitHub, a web browser, and more;
- **remember you** across chats;
- **remind you** of things and run tasks on a schedule;
- **talk and listen;**
- **see and make pictures;**
- serve your other machines from one place.

Your chats, memory and keys stay on your machine. API keys live in your OS keyring, never in a
file.
